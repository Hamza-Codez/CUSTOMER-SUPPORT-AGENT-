# Refund Policy

Aeron Home Goods refund terms for online orders.

## Refund window {#refund-window}

Refunds are available within 30 days of delivery, provided the item is unused and
in its original packaging. Refunds are issued to the original payment method and
take 5-10 business days to appear.

Orders placed as part of a clearance or final-sale promotion are excluded from the
standard refund window and are marked as such at checkout.

## Damaged or faulty goods {#damaged-goods}

If an item arrives damaged or develops a fault within 30 days, we replace or
refund it in full including original shipping. Photographs of the damage help us
process the claim faster, but are not required.

Damage caused after delivery — drops, spills, or modification of the product — is
not covered by this section. See the warranty policy for manufacturing defects
discovered later.
