# Warranty Policy

What Aeron Home Goods covers after the refund window closes.

## Warranty cover {#cover}

Desks and chairs carry a 5 year warranty on frames and mechanisms. Accessories
carry 1-2 years. Warranty covers manufacturing defects, not accidental damage or
normal wear.

## Making a warranty claim {#claims}

Warranty claims need the order number and a description of the fault. We may ask
for photographs or a short video of the mechanism. Approved claims are repaired or
replaced; we do not issue cash refunds under warranty once the refund window has
closed.
